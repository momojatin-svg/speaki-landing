Our Story

I lost my voice.

Years ago, I had a traumatizing experience of being embarrassed while speaking in front of others. That moment made me retreat into my own little corner — and slowly, quietly, I stopped speaking up. I had so much to say but I had learned to believe my voice wasn't worth the risk.

What changed? First, I said yes to working on my own personal growth. Second, I adjusted my circle. I surrounded myself with people who were passionate about their own growth and just as passionate about mine. I found my safe spaces. And from there, I practiced. Imperfectly. But consistently.

In the past 4 years, I have helped thousands of students find their own voice and get jobs, promotions, and improve the way they communicate in English — both at work and in their personal lives. I believe that when you work on your communication skills, all aspects of your life elevate.

That journey is why I'm now working with Kathy and Priyam.

Together, we built Speaki around some truths that non-native English speakers everywhere know all too well: the people who speak English with confidence get paid more, get promoted faster, and get taken more seriously.
"Your accent is not a flaw. Your voice deserves to be heard. Just speak. We'll be cheering you on."
— Rochelle, Kathy, and Priyam
