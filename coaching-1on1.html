Questions

Common questions

How long is a session? +
Each 1-on-1 session is 15 minutes for the video call option. For the video + audio feedback option, you send a recording of any length and receive a personalised audio response.
How do I choose between Rochelle and Kathy? +
Both mentors are excellent — it really comes down to personal preference. Rochelle focuses on identity shift and overcoming speaking fear. Kathy specialises in communication training and workplace English. You can try both over time!
What do I talk about in the session? +
Your mentor will guide you — but you can also come prepared with a specific topic, scenario (e.g. job interview, meeting, presentation), or question you want to work on. The session is entirely focused on you.
How long does audio feedback take? +
After you send your payment confirmation and video, expect your audio feedback within 24–48 hours on business days.
Where do I send my video recording? +
Send your proof of payment and video recording to hello@speaki.co or via our Facebook Messenger page. We'll confirm receipt and send your feedback within 24–48 hours.
Can I get a refund? +
Refunds are available within 24 hours of purchase if you haven't yet sent your video or booked your call. Please email hello@speaki.co to request one.
