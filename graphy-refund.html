Refund Policy

We want you to feel confident when you invest in your speaking journey.

24 Hour Refund Window

Refunds are available within 24 hours of purchase — no complicated process, no long wait.

Speaki is committed to your satisfaction. If for any reason you are not happy with your purchase, we make it easy to request a refund within 24 hours.

How to Request a Refund

1

Email us within 24 hours

Send your refund request to hello@speaki.co within 24 hours of your original purchase.

2

Include your purchase details

Include your name, the email address used for purchase, and the course or membership you would like refunded.

3

We process your refund

We will process your refund promptly. Refunds typically appear within 3–7 business days depending on your payment method and provider.

What's Eligible for a Refund

  • All course purchases requested within 24 hours of the original transaction
  • Membership purchases requested within 24 hours of the original transaction
  • Webinar or masterclass purchases within 24 hours, if the event has not yet taken place

What's Not Eligible

  • Refund requests made more than 24 hours after purchase
  • Webinars or masterclasses that have already taken place
  • Partial refunds for partially completed courses

Cancelling Your Membership

You may cancel your Speaki membership at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period — you will retain full access until then.

To cancel, log in to your account settings or contact us at hello@speaki.co.

Questions about a refund?
